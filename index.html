Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a makeover take?

A standard single-room makeover takes approximately 1 to 2 weeks from planning to final styling. A full-home beautification process usually takes between 4 to 8 weeks depending on the sourcing times and scale.

Do you work within my budget?

Yes, we work with a wide range of budgets. During our initial consultation, we discuss furniture choices and decor styles, setting clear allocations that match your goals without sacrificing the quality of results.

Can you redesign just one room?

Absolutely. We provide tailored room-by-room makeover services, color consultations, or organizational designs. We can focus specifically on your master bedroom, living room, entryway, or home office.

Do you shop for furniture?

Yes, our Shopping & Sourcing phase is fully managed by us. We handpick furniture, textiles, rugs, and decorative items. Through our vendor networks, we also secure exclusive trade discounts for our clients.

Can you manage contractors?

Yes, we provide full project coordination. While we are styling and design consultants, we coordinate with handymen, painters, lighting installers, and cleaning contractors to make sure the transformation runs smoothly.
